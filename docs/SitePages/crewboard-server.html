No complicated setup

One small server.
Your local network.

The device joins your Wi-Fi and serves files from its SD card. Your team connects from a browser or a WebDAV-compatible file manager.

  1. 1
    Add your network details

    Set up Wi-Fi and account details in the SD card’s configuration file.

  2. 2
    Connect the server

    Power it on. The display shows its network address and connection status.

  3. 3
    Share files with your crew

    Sign in from a browser or connect a compatible WebDAV client.
